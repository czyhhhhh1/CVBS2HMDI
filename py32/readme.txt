PY32F003 + SII9022A firmware
================================

Build:
  Keil MDK:  MDK-ARM/Project.uvprojx  (device: PY32F003x8)
  EIDE/GCC:  EIDE/Project.code-workspace

The PY32F003 configures the SII9022A over bit-banged I2C and sends
800x480 RGB888 from the AMT630A to HDMI.

See README.md for details.
